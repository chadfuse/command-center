I built a fully automated content distribution system that writes and publishes blog and social posts using only free services. No paid AI. No paid schedulers. No paid email service. Everything runs on Cloudflare Workers, Groq's free model, Resend, Unsplash, and free.ai. It was also built end-to-end with the free Devin SWE-1.7 coding agent, which handled every file, API integration, and debug session. What it does: ✅ Generates 1000+ word SEO blog posts with featured images ✅ Publishes to WordPress twice per week ✅ Publishes to LinkedIn, Facebook, and Instagram twice per day ✅ Pulls unique topics from RSS feeds and deduplicates them with Cloudflare KV ✅ Emails me a summary of every success or failure ✅ Falls back through Groq allam, qwen, and free.ai models if one fails The messy parts I had to solve: ⚠ The free 7B model often wrote short content, so I added an expansion loop ⚠ Free AI images were unpredictable, so I switched the image source to Unsplash first ⚠ Facebook and Instagram tokens expired every hour, so I built a helper endpoint to exchange them for long-lived Page tokens ⚠ Resend emails did not deliver until my domain was fully verified with DNS records ⚠ Instagram required a 10-second delay between media creation and publish It is not perfect. The AI cannot browse live news, the images are sometimes generic, and the rate limits keep me honest. But as a free, hands-off content engine, it is working.
